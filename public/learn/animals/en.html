Plant, fish, cat: different bodies, same jobs to do

Every living thing has to breathe, find food, move things around its body and hold itself up, but each one solves the same problems in very different ways. Pick a living thing, then tap a body system. Tap the numbers on the picture to read more.

Tap the same system again to see the whole body · A dashed system is one this living thing does not have. Tap it to find out why

RememberEvery living thing faces the same problems, but the answers depend on where it lives. A fish lives in water, so it breathes with gills. A cat lives on land, so it breathes with lungs. A plant stays in one place, so it makes its own food from light.
