Body 101 · Lesson 4 · ages 8 to 13

Growing every day, staying well

Our bodies never stop working. They grow, repair themselves and fight germs every single day. This lesson shows how the body changes from a baby onwards, and how we can help it by sleeping, eating, playing and washing our hands.

3 · EatThe 2:1:1 healthy plate

Fill the plate

Thailand's Department of Health recommends the 2:1:1 healthy plate: half vegetables, a quarter rice or other starch, and a quarter meat, fish, eggs or beans, plus fruit and milk or water. Tap foods to put them on the plate.

4 · Play60 minutes a day

Your body loves being used

The World Health Organization recommends that children play or exercise hard enough to get a bit out of breath for at least 60 minutes a day in total. Running, jumping, climbing and cycling all count.

Stronger bones

Every time you jump or run, your bones feel the impact and respond by building more bone to become stronger. Childhood is the best time in your whole life for building bone.

Heart, lungs and brain benefit too

The heart and lungs get stronger when they often work hard. After a good run around, the brain is more alert and remembers what it learns better, and you sleep better that night too.

5 · Handwashing7 steps · 20 seconds

Wash the germs away

Our hands touch things all day, so they pick up lots of germs. Scrub with soap through all 7 steps for at least 20 seconds. Press start and follow along.
